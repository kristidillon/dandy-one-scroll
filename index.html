Scan

AI-guided scanning, full arch in about 45 seconds

Real-time prep guidance flags margins, undercuts and clearance while you scan. No calibration. Dandy provides you with autoclavable scanner tips, and the scanner, laptop and cart are all included. The scanner learns your workflow, so accuracy keeps improving over time.
